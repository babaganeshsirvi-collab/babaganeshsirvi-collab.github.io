Policy review is pending. Online orders are not enabled.

Service and pricing

Baba's serves customers in Rajasthan, India. Dine-in receives 8% off and delivery receives 5% off the product subtotal. The final payable amount is shown before payment. Delivery requests are limited to Rajasthan, India. Provide a complete city, pincode and delivery address; serviceability is confirmed before fulfilment. Public browsing does not guarantee that every item can be fulfilled at every time.

Orders and payments

Payments use card or UPI through Razorpay when checkout is enabled. Cash payment is not offered on this website. An order is paid only after the payment provider confirms it. If payment status is pending, check your saved order before paying again. Acceptance and preparation status are shown separately from payment status.

Cancellation and refund

Contact support with your order reference to request cancellation or report an unavailable, incorrect or unfulfilled item. Requests are reviewed against the order's preparation and fulfilment status. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method. An order marked cancelled does not by itself confirm that a refund has been processed. Refund confirmation and timing are communicated after provider processing; this website does not promise a fixed bank settlement time.

Alcohol

Alcohol orders require customers to meet the applicable legal age and present valid identification when requested. A website age declaration does not replace an ID check. Alcohol delivery is enabled only for separately confirmed service areas and permissions. Service may be refused where the order cannot legally be fulfilled.

Privacy

Cart and favourites are stored in your browser. With OTP verification enabled, your mobile number and profile details are stored on the server. Orders retain product, payment reference and fulfilment details; delivery orders also retain the address you enter. Razorpay handles card and UPI credentials; Baba's does not store card numbers or UPI PINs. SMS delivery uses the configured verification provider. Essential session cookies keep sign-in secure.

You can remove cart and favourites through their controls and sign out from your profile. For access, correction or deletion of server-held details, contact support. Records needed for payment reconciliation or legal obligations may need to be retained. No advertising analytics are currently configured.

Support

A public support email must be configured before online orders are enabled.
